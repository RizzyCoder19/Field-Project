## 3. DETAILED ETHICAL DIRECTIVES

### Pillar 1: Voluntary Autonomy & Right to Withdraw
Participation in any survey, interview, or observational engagement is strictly voluntary. Every respondent is informed prior to participation that:
- They may refuse to answer any specific question without providing a reason.
- They may terminate the interaction at any point without penalty or loss of goodwill.
- No commercial, legal, or municipal incentives or coercions are offered for participation.

### Pillar 2: Informed Consent Protocol
Consent must be obtained prior to initiating any questionnaire or interview:
- **Verbal Consent (Rapid Surveys):** For rapid intercept questionnaires (`02_FIELD_WORK_QUESTIONNAIRE_SHORT.md`), the standardized verbal consent script must be read aloud, and the investigator must record the participant's explicit verbal agreement on the form header.
- **Written / Recorded Consent (Key Informant Interviews):** For in-depth semi-structured interviews (`03_FIELD_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md`), written signature or recorded verbal confirmation must be obtained using the formal consent section of `01_FIELD_WORK_QUESTIONNAIRE_FULL.md`.

### Pillar 3: Anonymity by Design (Zero PII Collection)
To protect participant confidentiality, **no Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** shall be solicited, collected, or stored:
- **Prohibited Data:** Full names, Aadhaar numbers, phone numbers, exact residential flat/house numbers, email addresses, or employer payroll numbers.
- **De-Identification Scheme:** All participants are assigned an alphanumeric token (e.g., `FW-RES-001`, `INT-ASHA-002`, `INT-CHEM-003`). The physical master linking key is kept in an encrypted, isolated directory and will be securely destroyed following final degree assessment.

### Pillar 4: Absolute Prohibition on Collecting Patient Clinical Records
Investigators must maintain a strict boundary between public health surveillance perceptions and private medical confidentiality:
- Investigators must **never** ask to view, copy, or photograph individual patient medical charts, hospital discharge summaries, laboratory diagnostic reports, or prescriptions.
- If a clinician or pharmacist references clinical patterns, discussions must remain strictly aggregated, general, and anonymized (e.g., *"We saw approximately 15 fever patients daily in July"*, rather than *"Patient X presented with platelet count Y"*).

### Pillar 5: Photographic Evidence Safeguards
Contextual photography serves to document physical environmental conditions (drainage, waste accumulation, public health banners). To ensure full compliance with privacy rights:
- **Strictly Prohibited:** Photographs of patient faces, hospital examination beds, children, residential interiors, or identifiable private property without formal written consent.
- **Permitted Subjects:** Public outdoor drainage infrastructure, road waterlogging, municipal warning banners, empty chemical abatement tags, public municipal building exteriors.
- Any accidental human capture in the background of a public photo must be digitally blurred or cropped prior to inclusion in the presentation or website.

### Pillar 6: Audio Recording Protocol
- Digital audio recording is conducted **solely and exclusively** upon obtaining explicit, affirmative verbal or written consent.
- If a participant declines audio recording, the digital recorder must remain powered off and stored out of sight. The investigator shall rely exclusively on written field notes.
- Audio recordings must be transcribed into de-identified text within 7 days, after which the raw audio files are stored in an encrypted offline volume.

### Pillar 7: Secure Local Data Storage & Chain of Custody
All physical and digital field assets must follow strict data custody standards:
- Physical questionnaires and handwritten field logs are cataloged in an indexed physical binder.
- Digitized CSV files (`06_FIELD_RESPONSE_TEMPLATE.csv`, `07_FIELD_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.csv`) must be stored on local password-protected workstations.
- Field data must **not** be uploaded to unvetted third-party public cloud services or public online repositories.

### Pillar 8: Anti-Fabrication & Academic Integrity Pledge
The credibility of the B.Sc. Data Science degree rests upon absolute empirical honesty:
- **Zero Tolerance for Fabrication:** Fabricating responses, simulating interviews, creating artificial survey data, inventing quotes, or misrepresenting reference materials as personal fieldwork is classified as severe academic misconduct under University of Mumbai ordinances.
- Any uncollected fieldwork must remain transparently labeled as `Planned / To Be Collected`.

### Pillar 9: Academic-Use Exclusivity
All collected data, transcripts, and photographic logs are collected solely for academic evaluation within the B.Sc. Data Science Semester III curriculum at RP Institute. The data shall not be monetized, sold, transferred to marketing entities, or utilized for political lobbying.

### Pillar 10: Non-Maleficence & Professional Community Conduct
- The student investigator is an academic observer, not a medical doctor or government inspector.
- Investigators must **never** offer medical diagnoses, contradict a doctor's advice, prescribe remedies, or guarantee that municipal action will follow a survey.
- Field interactions must be conducted with dignity, cultural respect, and sensitivity to community living conditions.
