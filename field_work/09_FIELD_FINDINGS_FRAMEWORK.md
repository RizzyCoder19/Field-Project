## THEME 1: COMMUNITY AWARENESS OF SEASONAL TIMING & ENVIRONMENTAL TRIGGERS

### 1. Evidentiary Inputs Required
- Responses from Full Questionnaire items 11–15 and Short Questionnaire items 6, 8, 9.
- Audio transcript excerpts from Core Interview Question 1.
- Transect weather logs and environmental risk scores from Observation Checklist Section 1 & 3.

### 2. Standardized Coding Categories
- `[TIMING_PRE_MONSOON]`: Awareness of early summer shifts (March–May).
- `[TIMING_MONSOON_BURST]`: Immediate perceived correlation with first seasonal downpours.
- `[TIMING_POST_MONSOON_LAG]`: Recognition of disease peaks lagging rainfall by 3–6 weeks.
- `[TRIGGER_WATER_STAGNATION]`: Perception that uncleaned stormwater drains drive mosquito spikes.
- `[TRIGGER_PIPELINE_CONTAMINATION]`: Perception that corroded drinking lines drive diarrhea.

### 3. Proposed Summarization Method
- Frequency cross-tabulation of perceived peak months against respondent residence duration.
- Illustrative verbatim quote matrix contrasting resident vs. clinician perceptions of early-onset indicators.

### 4. Direct Linkage to Frozen NCDC/IDSP Analysis
- Compares perceived community peak windows with empirical 2022–2025 quantitative peak concentration windows:
  - Dengue: 82.8% in June–October (Phase 5B/5C).
  - Malaria: 51.4% in May–July (Phase 5B/5C).
  - Acute Diarrheal Disease: 43.1% in June–August (Phase 5B/5C).
- Tests whether the early-monsoon concentration in Malaria (May–July) versus late-monsoon Dengue concentration (August–October) is intuitively recognized on the ground.

### 5. Explicit Analytical Boundaries (What CANNOT Be Concluded)
- *Cannot conclude* that community perception proves meteorological causation.
- *Cannot conclude* that absence of perceived fever equals absence of actual viral circulation.

## THEME 2: DISEASE-SPECIFIC PHENOMENOLOGY & SYMPTOM RECOGNITION

### 1. Evidentiary Inputs Required
- Full Questionnaire items 16–19, 22–25, 28–30.
- Short Questionnaire items 7, 8, 10.
- Interview transcripts from Clinicians, Pharmacists, and Pathologists (Core Questions 2, 3, 7).

### 2. Standardized Coding Categories
- `[DX_DENGUE_PLATELET]`: Recognition centered strictly around laboratory platelet counts.
- `[DX_MALARIA_CHILLS]`: Recognition centered around classic shivering/rigors.
- `[DX_CHIKUNGUNYA_ARTHRALGIA]`: Long-term joint incapacitation distinguishing Chikungunya.
- `[DX_ADD_WATER_BURST]`: Sudden acute gastrointestinal illness affecting households simultaneously.
- `[DX_FOOD_POISONING_POINT]`: Acute mass vomiting linked to a single common meal.

### 3. Proposed Summarization Method
- Percentage distribution of diseases witnessed across respondent categories.
- Qualitative matrix comparing frontline diagnostic criteria (syndromic vs. rapid test vs. full lab confirmation).

### 4. Direct Linkage to Frozen NCDC/IDSP Analysis
- Connects directly to the Phase 5A Disease Audit finding that 45.3% of 2026 surveillance records were labeled "Suspected" rather than confirmed.
- Explains why Chikungunya exhibits low absolute outbreak records (43 baseline records) compared to Dengue (204 records), potentially due to clinical conflation under general "viral fever" or "dengue-like illness".

### 5. Explicit Analytical Boundaries (What CANNOT Be Concluded)
- *Cannot conclude* clinical diagnostic accuracy or pathogen etiology from community interviews.
- *Cannot estimate* true epidemiological incidence ratios between diseases.

## THEME 3: PERCEIVED PUBLIC-HEALTH INTERVENTIONS & MUNICIPAL TIMELINESS

### 1. Evidentiary Inputs Required
- Full Questionnaire items 21, 27, 37, 39, 44.
- Short Questionnaire items 13, 14, 20.
- Key Informant interviews with Municipal Ward Officers and Sanitary Inspectors (Core Question 9).
- Observation Checklist Section 5 (Visible vector control marks, chemical spray tags, IEC posters).

### 2. Standardized Coding Categories
- `[RESP_PROACTIVE_EARLY]`: Evidence of larviciding or drain clearing conducted in April–May.
- `[RESP_REACTIVE_COMPLAINT]`: Evidence that fogging or inspection occurs only after resident complaints or deaths.
- `[RESP_IEC_VISIBILITY]`: Visibility and quality of municipal warning hoardings and stencils.
- `[RESP_SUPPLY_DISTRIBUTION]`: Field distribution of chlorine tablets (halazone) and ORS.

### 3. Proposed Summarization Method
- Likert-scale rating distribution of municipal response speed across settlement typologies (informal vs. planned).
- Spatial matrix comparing observed physical evidence (spray marks, banners) with resident satisfaction scores.

### 4. Direct Linkage to Frozen NCDC/IDSP Analysis
- Evaluates the operational feasibility of the Phase 5D & 5E surveillance pre-positioning windows:
  - Dengue: Recommended pre-positioning in Weeks 22–24 (early June).
  - Malaria: Recommended pre-positioning in Weeks 18–20 (early May).
- Identifies whether current municipal cycles align with these empirical windows or lag behind peak disease onset.

### 5. Explicit Analytical Boundaries (What CANNOT Be Concluded)
- *Cannot conclude* institutional negligence or audit municipal municipal budgets.
- *Cannot measure* biological efficacy of chemical insecticides or larvicidal formulations.

## THEME 4: COMMUNITY SURVEILLANCE LITERACY & PROGRAM RECOGNITION

### 1. Evidentiary Inputs Required
- Full Questionnaire items 33, 34, 38.
- Short Questionnaire items 16, 17.
- Key Informant interviews with Community Leaders, School Teachers, and Residents (Core Question 10).

### 2. Standardized Coding Categories
- `[SURV_AWARE_IDSP]`: Direct recognition of IDSP or NCDC by name or function.
- `[SURV_AWARE_GENERIC]`: General awareness that "government tracks fevers" without system knowledge.
- `[SURV_UNAWARE]`: Total absence of knowledge regarding public disease surveillance.
- `[SURV_INFO_SOURCE]`: Channels of outbreak news (WhatsApp, local newspaper, ASHA visit, clinic).

### 3. Proposed Summarization Method
- Binary/categorical awareness proportions stratified by education level and professional role.
- Sankey or narrative flow diagram mapping primary information channels used by residents.

### 4. Direct Linkage to Frozen NCDC/IDSP Analysis
- Bridges the public perception of government surveillance with the reality of official weekly reporting.
- Highlights that while IDSP is the cornerstone of state epidemiological defense, it remains largely invisible to the ordinary citizens whose illnesses it aggregates.

### 5. Explicit Analytical Boundaries (What CANNOT Be Concluded)
- *Cannot conclude* that low citizen awareness impairs internal epidemiological reporting by health staff.

## THEME 5: FRONTLINE NOTIFICATION PATHWAYS & REPORTING FRICTION

### 1. Evidentiary Inputs Required
- Full Questionnaire items 32, 35, 36.
- Short Questionnaire item 15.
- Key Informant interviews with Clinicians, ASHA workers, Chemists, and PHC Officers (Core Questions 4, 5, 6, 12).

### 2. Standardized Coding Categories
- `[PATH_PRIVATE_LEAKAGE]`: Tendency of patients to visit private GPs whose cases are never formally notified.
- `[PATH_OTC_CHEMIST]`: Direct self-medication through retail chemists bypassing clinical record-keeping.
- `[PATH_FORMAL_IHIP]`: Successful digital entry into IHIP/IDSP weekly reporting forms.
- `[BOTTLENECK_ADMIN_BURDEN]`: Clinicians reporting excessive paperwork or cumbersome digital forms.
- `[BOTTLENECK_LAB_DELAY]`: Inability to obtain rapid confirmation leading to "Suspected" classification.

### 3. Proposed Summarization Method
- Process-flow mapping tracing a patient's journey from fever onset to weekly NCDC report inclusion.
- Thematic barrier matrix identifying primary points of data leakage in the reporting chain.

### 4. Direct Linkage to Frozen NCDC/IDSP Analysis
- Explains the profound analytical distinction enforced in Phase 5C, 5D, and 5E between:
  - *Outbreak events* (clusters reported to authorities meeting investigation thresholds).
  - *Reported cases* (cases enumerated during outbreak field investigations).
  - *True community incidence* (all infections occurring in the general population).
- Provides real-world evidence for why NCDC reports represent an apex sample of severe clusters.

### 5. Explicit Analytical Boundaries (What CANNOT Be Concluded)
- *Cannot estimate* the exact mathematical under-reporting multiplier (e.g., cannot state "cases are under-reported by 10x" without a formal capture-recapture epidemiological study).

## THEME 6: LOCAL ENVIRONMENTAL VULNERABILITY & MICRO-HOTSPOTS

### 1. Evidentiary Inputs Required
- Observation Checklist Sections 2, 3, 4 (Environmental and Sanitation audits).
- Full Questionnaire items 20, 24, 25, 43.
- Short Questionnaire items 11, 12, 20.
- Contextual non-identifiable infrastructure photographs (indexed in `07_FIELD_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.csv`).

### 2. Standardized Coding Categories
- `[ENV_DRAIN_CHOKED]`: Siltation, plastic blockage, or zero flow in roadside open nullahs.
- `[ENV_STORAGE_DOMESTIC]`: Household storage drums uncovered due to 48-hour intermittent municipal supply.
- `[ENV_CONSTRUCTION_SUMP]`: Untreated pooled curing water on building sites.
- `[ENV_WATER_CONTAMINATION]`: Cracked drinking pipes running through sewage gutters.

### 3. Proposed Summarization Method
- Environmental Risk Index (ERI) composite score tabulated across visited observation sites.
- Photo-essay documentation demonstrating structural environmental breeding grounds.

### 4. Direct Linkage to Frozen NCDC/IDSP Analysis
- Ground-truths the spatial concentration metrics established in Phase 5C (Spatial Gini Coefficients: ADD 0.584, Malaria 0.575, Dengue 0.444).
- Illustrates why specific districts or high-density wards report persistent recurring outbreaks year after year.

### 5. Explicit Analytical Boundaries (What CANNOT Be Concluded)
- *Cannot conclude* that a photographed drain is the direct source of a specific clinical Dengue case.
- *Cannot extrapolate* neighborhood micro-observations to entire district-wide geographies.

## THEME 7: DATA READINESS & COMMUNITY ATTITUDES TOWARD EARLY WARNING ALERTS

### 1. Evidentiary Inputs Required
- Full Questionnaire items 40, 41, 42.
- Short Questionnaire items 18, 19.
- Interview transcripts from Pharmacists, School Administrators, and Ward Leaders (Core Question 11).

### 2. Standardized Coding Categories
- `[TECH_PROACTIVE_PREP]`: Willingness to clear domestic breeding sites or boil water upon early alert.
- `[TECH_SUPPLY_OPTIMIZATION]`: Pharmacists/clinics pre-ordering IV fluids and testing kits.
- `[TECH_PANIC_CONCERN]`: Fears that disease forecasts could trigger neighborhood stigma or commercial loss.
- `[TECH_PREFERRED_CHANNEL]`: Preferred dissemination medium (WhatsApp, SMS, ward boards).

### 3. Proposed Summarization Method
- Proportional acceptance score for data-driven early warning systems.
- Stakeholder recommendation matrix detailing practical operational steps 4–6 weeks prior to monsoon onset.

### 4. Direct Linkage to Frozen NCDC/IDSP Analysis
- Directly informs the Actionable Recommendations developed in Phase 5E Slide 17 and `08_CONCLUSION_RECOMMENDATIONS.md`.
- Evaluates whether predictive data science models would find practical utility at the community level.

### 5. Explicit Analytical Boundaries (What CANNOT Be Concluded)
- *Cannot guarantee* compliance with early warning alerts.
- *Cannot evaluate* digital software usability without testing a deployed prototype.
