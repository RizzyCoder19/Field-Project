## INVESTIGATIVE PURPOSE & INSTRUCTIONS TO ENUMERATOR

### Objective
This comprehensive questionnaire is designed to gather primary contextual data from residents, community leaders, local healthcare providers, and frontline workers across selected localities in Maharashtra. Its objective is to capture real-world observations regarding the timing of disease surges, environmental sanitation conditions, community awareness of surveillance programs (such as IDSP), and reporting behaviors.

### Relationship to Quantitative Analysis
This instrument does **not** evaluate clinical efficacy or measure population epidemiological incidence. It serves as **qualitative contextual evidence** to complement, ground, and interpret the frozen secondary quantitative findings from the National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC) / Integrated Disease Surveillance Programme (IDSP) dataset (2022–2026).

### Administration Mode Key
- `[SURVEY]`: Structured questions suitable for self-administered or enumerator-assisted written response.
- `[INTERVIEW]`: Semi-structured questions designed for interactive probing during verbal discussions.
- `[OBSERVATION]`: Environmental and behavioral indicators noted directly by the investigator on-site.
