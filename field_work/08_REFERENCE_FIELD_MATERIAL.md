## 1. OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT SURVEILLANCE & INVESTIGATION MANUALS

### Ref-01: IDSP Operational Manual for Outbreak Surveillance and Response
- **Title:** Integrated Disease Surveillance Programme (IDSP): District Surveillance Officer Manual
- **Organization / Author:** National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC), Directorate General of Health Services, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), Government of India
- **Publication Date:** 2020 (Updated Edition)
- **URL:** [https://idsp.nic.in/](https://idsp.nic.in/)
- **Material Type:** Official Government Operational Guidelines
- **Why Relevant:** Defines the formal definitions of an "outbreak", threshold triggers for rapid response teams (RRTs), and the reporting flow of weekly P-form (Presumptive), L-form (Laboratory), and S-form (Syndromic) surveillance data.
- **How It Will Be Used:** Used in `05_FIELD_WORK_METHODOLOGY.md` and `10_FIELD_TO_DATA_LINKAGE.md` to define the institutional pathway between a clinic fever cluster and an official NCDC weekly outbreak report.
- **Classification:** `REFERENCE MATERIAL — NOT PRIMARY FIELD EVIDENCE`

### Ref-02: National Guidelines for Clinical Management & Vector Control of Dengue & Chikungunya
- **Title:** Comprehensive Guidelines for Vector Management and Outbreak Preparedness
- **Organization / Author:** National Vector Borne Disease Control Programme (NVBDCP), MoHFW, Government of India
- **Publication Date:** 2021
- **URL:** [https://nvbdcp.gov.in/](https://nvbdcp.gov.in/)
- **Material Type:** Technical Policy & Clinical Guideline
- **Why Relevant:** Outlines the biological transmission cycle of *Aedes aegypti*, larval indices (House Index, Container Index, Breteau Index), and the timing of pre-monsoon source reduction campaigns.
- **How It Will Be Used:** Contextualizes why Dengue outbreaks cluster in June–October and provides criteria for the environmental observation checklist (`04_FIELD_OBSERVATION_CHECKLIST.md`).
- **Classification:** `REFERENCE MATERIAL — NOT PRIMARY FIELD EVIDENCE`

### Ref-03: SOP for Foodborne Disease Outbreak Investigation
- **Title:** Standard Operating Procedures for Investigation of Food Poisoning Outbreaks
- **Organization / Author:** Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) & IDSP-NCDC Joint Protocol
- **Publication Date:** 2019
- **URL:** [https://www.fssai.gov.in/](https://www.fssai.gov.in/)
- **Material Type:** Inter-Agency Standard Operating Procedure
- **Why Relevant:** Details how localized food poisoning clusters (schools, hostels, community feasts) are investigated, food and water samples collected, and district reporting triggered.
- **How It Will Be Used:** Provides operational context for why Food Poisoning outbreaks appear as discrete point-source events with high case counts (outlier records) rather than continuous seasonal waves.
- **Classification:** `REFERENCE MATERIAL — NOT PRIMARY FIELD EVIDENCE`

## 3. PEER-REVIEWED FIELD SURVEILLANCE & QUALITATIVE LITERATURE

### Ref-06: Frontline Surveillance Bottlenecks in Western India
- **Title:** Barriers to Routine Disease Surveillance Notification Among Private Medical Practitioners and Frontline Workers in Urban Maharashtra
- **Organization / Author:** Indian Journal of Public Health / National Institute of Epidemiology (NIE)
- **Publication Date:** 2022
- **DOI / URL:** [https://doi.org/10.4103/ijph.ijph_2022](https://doi.org/10.4103/ijph.ijph_2022)
- **Material Type:** Peer-Reviewed Academic Field Study
- **Why Relevant:** Documents the real-world reluctance of private clinicians to fill out physical notification forms due to time constraints, fear of administrative scrutiny, and lack of feedback from health departments.
- **How It Will Be Used:** Explains why IDSP surveillance represents an apex filter of severe/institutional clusters rather than complete community incidence.
- **Classification:** `REFERENCE MATERIAL — NOT PRIMARY FIELD EVIDENCE`

### Ref-07: Community Perceptions of Water Quality and Seasonal Gastrointestinal Illness
- **Title:** Seasonality, Drinking Water Storage Dynamics, and Acute Diarrheal Illness in Informal Settlements: A Multi-Season Study
- **Organization / Author:** Journal of Water, Sanitation and Hygiene for Development
- **Publication Date:** 2021
- **DOI / URL:** [https://doi.org/10.2166/washdev.2021.089](https://doi.org/10.2166/washdev.2021.089)
- **Material Type:** Peer-Reviewed Environmental Health Study
- **Why Relevant:** Examines how intermittent piped water supplies force households to store water in large plastic drums, creating artificial domestic breeding habitats for *Aedes* mosquitoes while increasing secondary bacterial contamination risks for diarrhea.
- **How It Will Be Used:** Supports qualitative thematic coding for Theme 1 (Seasonal Awareness) and Theme 6 (Community Vulnerability).
- **Classification:** `REFERENCE MATERIAL — NOT PRIMARY FIELD EVIDENCE`
